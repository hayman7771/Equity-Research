# Equity Research & Market Intelligence

This repository contains equity research, market intelligence, valuation support materials, investment memo templates, and presentation artifacts organized for practical investment analysis workflows.

The work in this repository is designed to demonstrate:
- investment research structuring
- market and industry analysis thinking
- valuation/modeling support
- memo writing and communication quality
- presentation readiness for decision-making contexts

This repository is part of a broader 5-repository portfolio ecosystem covering:
- Quant Finance
- Data Science
- Equity Research / Market Intelligence
- Mathematics
- Portfolio Site / Published Research

---

## Purpose

This repository is designed to show applied capability across the equity research process, including:

- **Market and industry analysis**
- **Investment memo development**
- **Valuation model support**
- **Presentation and communication artifacts**
- **Structured research workflows for screening and diligence**

It is intended to be easy for recruiters, hiring managers, and collaborators to review quickly.

## Repository Contents

### 1) Industry Analysis
Frameworks and notes for evaluating industry dynamics and market opportunity.

**Includes:**
- Industry structure templates
- Competitive landscape analysis
- Market sizing frameworks
- Unit economics templates
- Sector-specific notes

**Why it matters:**  
These frameworks support disciplined top-down and bottom-up analysis before deep valuation work begins.

---

### 2) Investment Memos
Templates and examples for structuring investment arguments and screening opportunities efficiently.

**Includes:**
- One-page memo templates
- Quick-screen templates
- Case-study style memo examples
- Diligence-oriented writeups

**Why it matters:**  
Strong memo writing is critical for turning analysis into clear, decision-ready communication.

---

### 3) Models
Core financial and transaction support models used in valuation and deal analysis.

**Includes:**
- DCF modeling
- Capital structure analysis
- Scenario analysis
- Trading comps
- Transaction comps
- LBO-lite framework
- Model specification notes

**Why it matters:**  
This folder demonstrates practical valuation mechanics and modeling structure used in investment workflows.

---

### 4) Presentations
Presentation materials for communicating research and investment conclusions.

**Includes:**
- Equity research presentation template
- Example presentation artifacts

**Why it matters:**  
Strong presentations make research usable for investment committees, stakeholders, and interview settings.

---

### 5) Reports
Written research outputs and report-style templates.

**Includes:**
- Sample investment thesis template
- One-page memo examples

**Why it matters:**  
This section highlights clarity of thought, synthesis, and communication quality.
